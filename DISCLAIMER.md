## 1. What it contains

- **Research.** Course sheets, information on exams, rules, lecturers, timetables and calendars, past exam exercises with solutions: they come from public sources (the degree programme website, Moodle pages open to guests, the Esse3 exam listings, University Planner, the student guide of the Team Studentesco Informatica) and from some Moodle pages visible only with a UniTo login (Foundations of Computer Science channel B, MDAG, the Foundations of Computer Science exam page on Moodle Esami), accessed by me. The sources are cited and the main data have been checked by cross-referencing several sources.
- **Lesson notes.** They rework the lecturers' slides, lesson by lesson.
- **English translation.** This repository is the English translation of the Italian original ([DonFlammer/unito-informatica](https://github.com/DonFlammer/unito-informatica)), made for students who do not speak Italian. The translation may add errors of its own. The Italian version is updated first: if the two versions differ, **the Italian one prevails**.
- **Channel.** I follow **channel B** (surnames E–O) and the lesson notes are based on the channel B slides. The official programme and the exam are common to the three channels, so the notes should largely hold for A and C too, but lecturers, slides, topic order, examples and the parts of the programme actually covered may differ (for example, in Foundations of Computer Science channel B omits some sections of the book that the common map includes): for your own channel, only your lecturer's slides and Moodle page are authoritative. The course sheets cover all three channels.

In all cases the content is **valid and useful, but may still contain errors, omissions or outdated data**.
