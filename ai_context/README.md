# Context for AIs

> Italian original: <https://github.com/DonFlammer/unito-informatica/blob/main/contesto_ai/README.md>

This folder collects in Markdown everything an AI needs to help with the first year of Computer Science at UniTo (University of Turin) **without redoing the research from scratch**: how the degree programme is organised, how the exams work, the sheets of all the courses for channels A, B and C, the notes of each lesson and worked exam exercises. I, DonFlammer, maintain this collection and follow **channel B**: the lesson notes are based on the channel B slides; the official programme and the exam are common to the three channels, so they should largely hold for A and C too, but slides, topic order, examples and the parts of the programme actually covered may differ.

These files are the **English mirror** of the Italian original ([contesto_ai/](https://github.com/DonFlammer/unito-informatica/tree/main/contesto_ai) in DonFlammer/unito-informatica). New lessons appear in Italian first; if the two versions differ, the Italian one prevails. The courses themselves are taught in Italian: official names and terms are given in Italian in parentheses.

> **⚠️ Disclaimer.** The research (degree programme, course sheets, exams, exam exercises) comes from public sources and, for some sheets (Foundations of Computer Science, in particular channel B and the exam page on Moodle Esami, and MDAG), also from Moodle pages visible only with a UniTo login, accessed by me; the lesson notes rework the lecturers' slides. They are accurate and sourced, but may contain errors or outdated data. **I take no responsibility whatsoever**; whoever uses them does so at their own risk and must check important information on the official sources. I update the files lesson by lesson, nothing more. Full text: [../DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md).

## Quick start

**One file only:** attach `_ALL_IN_ONE.md`, which contains all the other files (it is regenerated at every update).

**Or only the files you need:** always `ai_instructions.md` and `student.md`, then `unito_computer_science.md` and the sheet of the course you are asking about (for example `PROG1/course.md` plus the lesson in `PROG1/lessons/`).

Prompt to paste together with the files:

```text
I'm attaching the context files of my studies (Computer Science, University of Turin, 1st year, A.Y. 2026/27, channel <A/B/C>).
Read ai_instructions.md first and follow its rules; use these files as your main source
instead of redoing the research, and tell me if anything looks outdated.
My request: <write your question here>
```
