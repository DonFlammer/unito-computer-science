First-year notes

Study notes lesson by lesson, with worked examples, simulators and exercises, plus sheets for every first-year course for channels A, B and C.

English mirror. This site is the English translation of the Italian original. New lessons appear there first; if the two versions differ, the Italian one prevails.

The first year is split into three channels by surname: A (A–D), B (E–O) and C (P–Z). I follow channel B: the lesson notes are based on the channel B slides, with references to the lessons of A and C. The official programme and the exam are common to the three channels, so the notes should largely hold for A and C too; slides, topic order, examples and the parts of the programme actually covered may differ, though, and for your channel only your lecturer's slides and Moodle page are authoritative.

Studying in Italian

International students

  • The courses are taught in Italian: the official 2026/27 course pages list Italian as the language of seven of the eight first-year courses (the eighth is English I). Lectures, slides and Moodle pages are in Italian, and so are the exams unless the lecturer allows English.
  • Two first-year courses are labelled English-friendly on their official 2026/27 pages: Foundations of Computer Science and Computer Architecture. The department defines English-friendly courses as “courses with available material in English to prepare the exam, and teacher allowing students to give the exam in English” (International students page). Ask the lecturer at the start of the course.
  • Official Italian names and terms are given in parentheses, so you can find them on Moodle, Esse3 and the degree programme website. Dates are written day/month/year, as in the Italian sources.

Additional learning requirement · maths

Below 5 out of 20 in Basic Mathematics on the TOLC-S?

Then you have the maths OFA (obbligo formativo aggiuntivo), to be cleared within the first year: until you pass it you cannot record second-year exams. There is a separate guide for it: verified rules and dates, notes for the eight modules of the official course, an entry test, timed mock tests and a study plan built around the date of your sitting.
