Discrete Mathematics, Algebra and Geometry

Official Italian name: Matematica Discreta, Algebra e Geometria

I follow channel B: the notes for each lesson are based on the channel B material (slides, handouts or textbook), with references to channels A and C. The official programme and the exam are common, so they largely hold for A and C too, but slides, order, examples and the parts of the programme covered may differ. Lecturers, timetables, Moodle and exam for all three channels are in the course sheet.

The course has two parts, with separate lessons, Moodle pages and written exams: part 1 is Discrete Mathematics, part 2 is Linear Algebra and Geometry. In the timetable (University Planner) part 1 is called modA and part 2 modB; on Moodle they are MDAG1 and MDAG2. The part 2 notes already cover all 26 lessons of the 2026 handouts, the same for the three channels: they are ready ahead of time, so the pace in class may differ. Part 1 also has a programme and an exam common to the three channels: its notes follow the channel B lessons (Mori, with his book) and arrive lesson by lesson.

Exam
Two separate written exams, part 1 (modA, Discrete Mathematics) and part 2 (modB, Linear Algebra and Geometry); the grade is the average.
Exam dates
Part 1 (modA, Discrete Mathematics) 19/01 and 03/02, part 2 (modB, Geometry) 22/01 and 05/02/2027
