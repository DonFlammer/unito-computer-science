Week 1: the real numbers

The lesson of the week in a few pages: the four families of numbers, the nine rules of arithmetic, the brackets and the rules for roots, with the questions to check yourself.

Lessons
L01, in channel B on Thursday 01/10
Handouts
Buzano and Radeschi 2026, pp. 2–5

20–30 minutes

The week in 5 points

  • Numbers are four families, one inside the other: N⊂Z⊂Q⊂R\N \subset \Z \subset \Q \subset \RN⊂Z⊂Q⊂R.
  • Some real numbers are not fractions: they are irrational, like 2\sqrt 22​. That it is not a fraction is proved by contradiction.
  • A field is a set of numbers in which the nine rules of arithmetic hold: Q\QQ, R\RR and C\CC yes, N\NN and Z\ZZ no.
  • Curly, round and square brackets say different things.
  • In the exam there is no calculator: roots are simplified by hand.

L01 · The real numbers

  • Each family is inside the next and has something more: N⊊Z⊊Q⊊R\N \subsetneq \Z \subsetneq \Q \subsetneq \RN⊊Z⊊Q⊊R.
  • A fraction has many forms: 12=24=36\frac12 = \frac24 = \frac3621​=42​=63​. Fractions are the decimals that end or repeat; those that never repeat are irrational.
  • An oddity: 0.999…=10.999\ldots = 10.999…=1, two ways of writing the same number.
  • Precisely, a real number is a list of fractions that get closer and closer to each other: useful for understanding, not for the exam. R\RR is complete, that is it has no gaps; Q\QQ does: (1+1n)n\left(1 + \frac1n\right)^n(1+n1​)n rises towards e≈2.718e \approx 2.718e≈2.718, which is not a fraction.

Why 2\sqrt 22​ is not a fraction. 2\sqrt 22​ is the diagonal of a square with side 1, by Pythagoras. You reason by contradiction:

  1. pretend that 2=ab\sqrt 2 = \frac ab2​=ba​, with the fraction already in lowest terms;
  2. then a2=2b2a^2 = 2b^2a2=2b2, so a2a^2a2 is even and aaa is even too: a=2ka = 2ka=2k;
  3. substituting, 4k2=2b24k^2 = 2b^24k2=2b2, that is b2=2k2b^2 = 2k^2b2=2k2: bbb is even too;
  4. but then the fraction could still be simplified by 2. Contradiction: 2\sqrt 22​ is not a fraction.

Pitfall · Irrational times irrational

The product of two irrational numbers is not always irrational: 2⋅2=2\sqrt 2 \cdot \sqrt 2 = 22​⋅2​=2.

The nine rules of arithmetic. For addition: associative, commutative, there is 0, every number has an opposite. For multiplication: associative, commutative, there is 1, every number different from 0 has an inverse. Plus the distributive law, a(b+c)=ab+aca(b + c) = ab + aca(b+c)=ab+ac.

Definition · Field

A set of numbers with addition and multiplication in which all nine rules hold.

How to read it. In practice a field is a set in which you can do the four operations without leaving it. Q\QQ, R\RR and C\CC are fields; N\NN is not, because −1-1−1 is missing; Z\ZZ is not, because 12\frac1221​ is missing. Zero has no inverse: you do not divide by zero.

Order. a>ba > ba>b means that a−ba - ba−b is positive, that is aaa lies further right on the number line. N\NN, Z\ZZ, Q\QQ and R\RR are ordered; C\CC is not.

Pitfall · "If … then" does not hold backwards

If a=2a = 2a=2 then a2=4a^2 = 4a2=4; but a2=4a^2 = 4a2=4 does not give a=2a = 2a=2, because a=−2a = -2a=−2 works too. When a sentence holds both ways one says "if and only if".

Method · Calculations with roots without a calculator

  1. a⋅b=ab\sqrt a \cdot \sqrt b = \sqrt{ab}a​⋅b​=ab​, and a square comes out of the root: 12=4⋅3=23\sqrt{12} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 3} = 2\sqrt 312​=4⋅3​=23​.
  2. Only equal roots add up: 23+53=732\sqrt 3 + 5\sqrt 3 = 7\sqrt 323​+53​=73​, while 2+3\sqrt 2 + \sqrt 32​+3​ stays as it is.
  3. To remove the root from the denominator multiply top and bottom by that root: 13=33\frac1{\sqrt 3} = \frac{\sqrt 3}33​1​=33​​.
  4. a+b\sqrt{a + b}a+b​ is not a+b\sqrt a + \sqrt ba​+b​: 9+16=5\sqrt{9 + 16} = 59+16​=5, while 3+4=73 + 4 = 73+4=7.

§3

Review questions

self-check

Answer out loud or in writing, then open the answer.

Is −4-4−4 in N\NN? In Z\ZZ? In Q\QQ?

It is not in N\NN; it is in Z\ZZ and in Q\QQ.

Is 0.333…0.333\ldots0.333… rational?

Yes: it is 13\frac1331​, the digits repeat.

Is Z\ZZ a field? Why?

No: 2 has no inverse in Z\ZZ, because 12\frac1221​ is not an integer.

What is the difference between (2,5)(2, 5)(2,5) and [2,5][2, 5][2,5]? Is 5 in both?

The first excludes the endpoints, the second includes them: 5 is only in [2,5][2, 5][2,5].

Simplify 50\sqrt{50}50​ and 62\frac6{\sqrt 2}2​6​.

50=25⋅2=52\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \cdot 2} = 5\sqrt 250​=25⋅2​=52​; 62=622=32\frac6{\sqrt 2} = \frac{6\sqrt 2}2 = 3\sqrt 22​6​=262​​=32​.

Is 2⋅8\sqrt 2 \cdot \sqrt 82​⋅8​ irrational?

No: 2⋅8=16=4\sqrt 2 \cdot \sqrt 8 = \sqrt{16} = 42​⋅8​=16​=4.
